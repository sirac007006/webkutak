ITS Webshop — online IT equipment store

A complete webshop for the Podgorica IT store ITS — a catalog of nearly 900 products that fills itself from supplier feeds, a cart and checkout without registration, and an admin panel that runs the whole store.

Visit site Back to projects

About the project

ITS (Information Technology Service) is an IT store and authorized service center in Podgorica selling laptops, computers, monitors, phones and peripherals. They needed a webshop that doesn't require hand-entering hundreds of products and works as a real business tool, not just a storefront.

We built it from scratch in Astro with server-side rendering and a PostgreSQL database. The catalog is filled automatically through the suppliers' B2B services (PIN Computers and Mainframe) — products, purchase prices and availability arrive on their own, and the selling price is calculated from the margin the store sets. Prices, stock and shipping are always calculated on the server, so a price change in the admin applies immediately, even to a cart already sitting in someone's browser.

Beyond the store, ITS gets a complete back office: orders with statuses, customer records, sales reports, a store budget, quotes for businesses, review moderation and an Instagram post generator straight from the catalog. A React Native app for store staff and delivery drivers was built alongside the site.

Features

  • Catalog of ~900 products with categories, groups and curated collections
  • Server-side filters — a shared link instantly shows the exact result
  • Real-time search suggestions (typeahead)
  • Cart, wishlist and checkout as a guest or with an account
  • Stock check and order creation in a single transaction — the last item can't be sold twice
  • Automatic supplier import (SOAP/XML B2B services) with margin calculation
  • Admin dashboard: revenue, charts, orders, customers, products and suppliers
  • Reports by period with CSV export (Excel compatible)
  • Quotes for companies exported to PDF and Word
  • Instagram post generator (1:1, 4:5, 9:16) from product data
  • Google Merchant Center feed, dynamic sitemap and structured data for SEO
  • React Native app for store staff and delivery drivers

Result

ITS got an online store that practically maintains itself: products and prices update automatically, orders land straight in the admin panel, and products appear on Google Shopping through the Merchant feed. The site is live at itscg.me.

Technologies

Astro TypeScript Node.js PostgreSQL React Native SOAP / XML
