About the project

A complete digital solution for the service centers of the VOX Electronics network in Montenegro. The app automatically pulls in orders from email, assigns them to technicians and tracks every step — from intake to delivery.

Features

  • Three roles: admin, technician, driver
  • Real-time order status tracking
  • GPS driver tracking
  • Statistics, earnings and rankings
  • Push and email notifications
  • Admin panel for full control

Technologies

React Native Node.js Express PostgreSQL JavaScript
